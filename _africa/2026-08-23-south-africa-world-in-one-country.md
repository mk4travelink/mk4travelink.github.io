## 🏔️ Cape Town & The Mother City's Icons

No visit is complete without experiencing the breathtaking **Table Mountain**. Take the rotating cable car to its flat summit for panoramic views of the city, ocean, and surrounding peaks. Just offshore lies **Robben Island**, a UNESCO World Heritage site and the former prison where Nelson Mandela was held for 18 years—a powerful symbol of South Africa's journey to freedom. Explore the colourful houses of **Bo-Kaap**, spot adorable African penguins at **Boulders Beach**, and drive along the spectacular Cape Peninsula to the legendary **Cape of Good Hope**. The **Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden**, named one of the most beautiful gardens in the world, showcases the unique Cape Floral Region, a UNESCO World Heritage site in its own right.

## 🐘 Kruger National Park – Safari of a Lifetime

As one of Africa's greatest wildlife reserves, **Kruger National Park** is the ultimate destination for safari enthusiasts. Spanning nearly 20,000 square kilometres of untamed bushveld, it boasts the widest variety of classic safari animals, including the legendary **Big Five**—lion, leopard, elephant, rhino, and buffalo. Whether on a self-drive adventure or a guided game drive with expert rangers, every sunrise brings the thrill of discovery in this unparalleled wilderness.

## 🌿 The Garden Route – A Scenic Masterpiece

Stretching from Mossel Bay to the Storms River mouth, this legendary coastal drive is one of the world's most beautiful road trips. Dense indigenous forests, pristine beaches, and charming towns like **Knysna** and **Plettenberg Bay** line the route. For adrenaline seekers, the **Bloukrans Bridge** offers the world's highest commercial bungee jump at 216 metres, while the ancient **Cango Caves** near Oudtshoorn invite exploration of their underground chambers. Hike to the iconic **Storms River Suspension Bridge** in Tsitsikamma National Park, where the forest meets the sea.
