## 🏛️ The Pyramids of Giza & The Great Sphinx

No trip to Egypt is complete without standing before the last surviving Wonder of the Ancient World. Rising from the desert after more than 4,500 years, the three Great Pyramids—Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure—continue to inspire awe and mystery. The Great Pyramid alone was constructed from over 2 million stone blocks, each weighing between 2.5 and 15 tons. Guarding this ancient necropolis is the enigmatic Sphinx, carved from a single limestone outcrop, with its lion's body and pharaoh's head watching over Giza for millennia. The nearby Valley Temple showcases ancient Egyptian masonry at its finest, with massive granite blocks fitted together without mortar.

## 🏛️ The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)

A new chapter in Egypt's tourism renaissance, the GEM officially opened in 2025 and is the largest museum in the world dedicated to a single civilisation. Located just outside Cairo near the pyramids, it houses over 100,000 artifacts—including the complete collection from King Tutankhamun's tomb, presented together for the first time. This extraordinary museum is already attracting about 15,000 visitors daily and has become a major driver of Egypt's tourism boom.

## 🏛️ Luxor – The World's Greatest Open-Air Museum

Across the Nile from modern Luxor lies the Valley of the Kings, the royal burial ground of Egypt's most powerful pharaohs from the New Kingdom period. Sixty-three tombs have been discovered here, including the famous tomb of Tutankhamun, whose treasures now reside in the GEM. Each tomb is carved deep into rock and decorated with vivid wall paintings depicting the pharaoh's journey through the afterlife. Nearby, the Karnak Temple complex stands as the largest religious site ever constructed, featuring 134 giant stone columns arranged like a forest, with sunlight streaming through the gaps and illuminating densely carved hieroglyphs and reliefs. Just a short distance away, Luxor Temple comes alive at night, illuminated by lights that highlight its towering obelisks.

In Aswan, you can also board a traditional felucca—a wind-powered sailboat—for a serene ride past lush palm groves. ## 🐠 The Red Sea Riviera

Beyond the ancient wonders lies a different kind of paradise. The Red Sea resorts of Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, and Marsa Alam offer world-class beaches and some of the best diving and snorkelling on the planet. Explore vibrant coral reefs teeming with colourful marine life at Ras Mohammed National Park, or for experienced divers, the famous Blue Hole in Dahab offers thrilling wreck dives. Egypt's unprecedented tourism surge in 2025 welcomed approximately 19 million visitors, with these coastal destinations ranking among the top airports receiving tourists.

## 📅 Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is during the cooler months from **October to April**, with October, November, February, and March offering the perfect balance of comfortable temperatures (20–28°C / 68–82°F), manageable crowds, and lower prices. Early morning visits to outdoor archaeological sites are highly recommended for the best lighting and cooler conditions.
