## 🏯 The Great Wall of China

No trip to China is complete without walking along this epic testament to human endeavour, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Stretching more than 20,000 kilometres, its various sections offer different experiences—from the well-restored and accessible Mutianyu to the rugged hiking trails of Jinshanling. As you stand atop these ancient battlements, you will understand why it remains the ultimate symbol of China.

## 🏛️ The Forbidden City

In the heart of Beijing lies the largest and best-preserved ancient palace complex in the world. Home to 24 Ming and Qing emperors for over 500 years, this sprawling masterpiece of red walls, golden tiles, and grand halls offers a glimpse into the opulent lives of China's historic rulers.

## 🗿 The Terracotta Army

Buried for over 2,200 years, this silent legion of life-sized warriors stands guard over the tomb of China's first emperor in Xi'an. Each warrior has unique facial features, making this UNESCO World Heritage Site one of the most impressive historical discoveries in the world.

## 🌄 Beyond These Icons, China's Wonders Seem Endless:

- **The Li River in Guilin** – Drift through a landscape of emerald waters and towering karst mountains that look like a traditional Chinese ink painting come to life.

- **Chengdu's Giant Panda Breeding Base** – Get up close with China's beloved national treasures in the world's largest panda breeding centre.

- **Zhangjiajie** – Walk among the surreal stone pillars that inspired the floating mountains in the movie Avatar.

- **The Bund in Shanghai** – Witness the breathtaking contrast of historic colonial architecture against the futuristic skyscrapers of Pudong.
