# 📝 Plan Your Perfect Trip with Tripadvisor

Planning a trip can be overwhelming. Endless options, conflicting reviews, and the fear of making the wrong choice can turn excitement into anxiety.

**Tripadvisor** combines the wisdom of millions of real travelers with cutting-edge AI to help you plan every detail of your journey.

## 🌍 What is Tripadvisor?

Tripadvisor is the world's largest travel guidance platform. With over **one billion reviews and contributions** from travelers worldwide, it helps millions of people discover, plan, and book the perfect trip.

## ✨ Why Travelers Choose Tripadvisor

### 1. Real Reviews from Real Travelers
Over one billion reviews and contributions give you honest, unfiltered feedback from travelers just like you.

### 2. AI-Powered Trip Planning
- **AI Trip Planner** – Build personalized itineraries through conversational prompts.
- **ChatGPT Integration** – Access reviews, ratings, and images directly within your chat.
- **Perplexity Partnership** – Get personalized, actionable recommendations.

### 3. Travelers' Choice Awards
Each year, Tripadvisor highlights the highest-rated attractions and experiences — winners are among the top **10%** of listings globally.

- **Basílica de la Sagrada Familia**, Barcelona

### 4. Plan and Book Everything in One Place
- Create an itinerary with the **Trips** tool.
- Book flights, hotels, and experiences all in one place.
- Find traveler-recommended places nearby on a map.
- Get custom recommendations based on your saves.

### 5. Tripadvisor Rewards
Earn **Trip Cash** that can be redeemed on hotel and activity bookings.

## 🚀 How to Get Started

1. **Search** – Enter your destination and explore millions of reviews.
2. **Compare** – Filter by price, rating, location, and traveler preferences.
3. **Plan** – Use the AI Trip Planner to build personalized itineraries.
4. **Book** – Reserve your stay, activities, and dining in one place.
5. **Go!** – Travel with confidence, knowing you've made informed choices.
