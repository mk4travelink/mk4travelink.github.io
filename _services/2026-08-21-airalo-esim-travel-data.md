## 📱 What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is an **embedded SIM card** — a tiny digital chip built into your phone's hardware that functions exactly like a physical SIM, but 100% digitally. You simply download an eSIM profile directly to your phone and connect to a mobile network within minutes.
