## 🏔️ The Matterhorn & Zermatt

No visit to Switzerland is complete without witnessing its most famous landmark. The iconic, pyramid-shaped Matterhorn towers over the car-free village of Zermatt, offering endless views of this majestic peak. Whether hiking scenic trails, skiing on Europe's highest slopes, or riding the Gornergrat Railway for sunrise over 29 peaks, Zermatt delivers an alpine experience like no other.

## ❄️ Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe

Ascend to Europe's highest railway station at 3,454 metres and stand in awe of a world of eternal snow and ice. The Jungfraujoch offers a stunning Sphinx observatory, a glacier viewing platform, and the breathtaking Aletsch Glacier—a UNESCO Natural World Heritage site and the largest glacier in the Alps. This high-alpine destination is a jewel in the crown of any Swiss itinerary.

## 🌉 Lucerne & The Chapel Bridge

Nestled between the mountains and Lake Lucerne, this picturesque city is home to Europe's oldest wooden covered bridge, the 15th-century Kapellbrücke (Chapel Bridge), and a beautifully preserved medieval Old Town. Ride a cable car to Mount Pilatus for panoramic views of over 70 Alpine peaks, or explore the Swiss Museum of Transport, the country's most visited museum.

## 🌊 Beyond These Icons, Switzerland Reveals Countless More Treasures:

- The thunderous **Rhine Falls**—Europe's largest waterfall.
- The spectacular **Château de Chillon**, a 12th-century water castle on Lake Geneva.
- The UNESCO-listed **Lavaux Vineyard Terraces** stretching along the lake's shores.
- The adventure hub of **Interlaken**, nestled between turquoise Lake Brienz and stunning Lake Thun, offering paragliding, skydiving, and hiking.
