## What is Expedia?

Expedia is one of the world's leading online travel agencies, offering a comprehensive platform for booking flights, hotels, car rentals, cruise ships, and vacation packages. Launched in 1996 as a small startup inside Microsoft, Expedia has evolved across three major technology waves—from the dawn of the internet through the mobile shift and now into the AI era. Today, it operates one of the world's most trusted and intelligent travel marketplaces, connecting millions of travelers with partners across lodging, air, car, and activities.

Expedia Group's portfolio includes over 200 travel booking websites, including Hotels.com, Vrbo, Orbitz, Trivago, and Travelocity, serving 75 countries in 35 languages with over 600 million monthly visits. Its flagship **One Key** loyalty program unifies rewards across Expedia, Hotels.com, and Vrbo, creating a seamless experience for travelers.
