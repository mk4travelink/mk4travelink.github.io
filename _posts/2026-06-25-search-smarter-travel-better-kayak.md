## What is KAYAK?

KAYAK is the world's leading travel search engine. Founded in 2004 by Steve Hafner and Paul English, KAYAK searches hundreds of travel sites at once to show you all your options in one place. Whether you're looking for flights, hotels, rental cars, or vacation packages, KAYAK brings together results from airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, and car rental companies side by side.

Part of Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG), KAYAK processes billions of queries across its platforms and is trusted by millions of travelers worldwide. The company also manages a portfolio of travel search brands including SWOODOO, checkfelix, momondo, Cheapflights, Mundi, and HotelsCombined.

## Why Travelers Choose KAYAK

### 1. Search Everything in One Place

KAYAK is a true **all-in-one travel search engine**. Instead of hopping between different websites, you can compare flights, hotels, rental cars, and vacation packages in a single search. Results pages come with loads of filter options to help you find exactly what you're looking for and make booking seamless.

### 2. AI-Powered Conversational Search

KAYAK is leading the travel industry's AI revolution with innovative tools that make trip planning faster and more intuitive.

**AI Mode**: Launched in October 2025, AI Mode is a natural-language search experience powered by ChatGPT that allows users to plan trips by typing questions in everyday language. KAYAK is the first major travel site to let users plan complete trips by typing questions directly into a search box. Simply click the "AI Mode" icon, type travel-related questions the way you'd say them to another person, and get real-time results as KAYAK compares the latest information and prices from hundreds of travel providers.

**Ask AI**: Building on AI Mode, KAYAK introduced Ask AI—an industry-first conversational travel planning experience that lets travelers start planning their trip in a chat while flight, hotel, and rental car results update live alongside the conversation. Search for hotels near a stadium, compare flights between cities, or build your full itinerary—all without switching tabs.

### 3. Real-Time Pricing and Price Tracking

KAYAK gives you the tools, deals, and data you need to compare options and book with confidence. The platform offers:
- **Real-time pricing** in conversational search—the only platform that offers this (others rely on cached data)
- **PriceCheck**: Take a screenshot of any flight itinerary and compare it against hundreds of travel sites to ensure you're getting the best price
- **Wishlist**: Save your next adventures and track prices over time
- **Price Alerts**: Get notified when prices drop for your preferred routes

### 5. KAYAK Trips: Your All-in-One Travel Organizer

KAYAK Trips creates a travel itinerary for you with flight status alerts, gate changes, luggage carousel information, and boarding passes—all accessible on and offline. Simply forward your booking confirmations or sync your email account to keep all your travel plans organized in one app, even if you didn't book with KAYAK.

### 6. Book with Confidence

When you're ready to book, KAYAK connects you directly to the provider—whether that's an airline, hotel, or car rental company. KAYAK doesn't handle the booking itself; it connects you to the partner to complete your reservation. This means transparent pricing with no hidden middleman fees.
