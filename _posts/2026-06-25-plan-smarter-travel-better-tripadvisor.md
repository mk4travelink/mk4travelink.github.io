## What is Tripadvisor?

Tripadvisor is the world's largest travel guidance platform. With over **one billion reviews and contributions** from travelers worldwide, it helps millions of people discover, plan, and book the perfect trip. Whether you're looking for hotels, restaurants, flights, or experiences, Tripadvisor brings together trusted guidance from real travelers to help you make confident decisions.

## Why Travelers Choose Tripadvisor

### 1. Real Reviews from Real Travelers

Tripadvisor is built on the power of community. With over one billion reviews and contributions, you get honest, unfiltered feedback from travelers just like you. Access millions of reviews about hotels, restaurants, tours, attractions, and other experiences—all from people who have been there.

### 2. AI-Powered Trip Planning

Tripadvisor is leading the travel industry's AI revolution with innovative tools that make trip planning faster, smarter, and more personalized.

- **AI Trip Planner**: A generative AI-powered tool that helps travelers build personalized itineraries through conversational prompts. Simply ask questions like "What are the best things to do in Lisbon this weekend with kids?" and get tailored recommendations instantly.

- **ChatGPT Integration**: Tripadvisor has launched an app on ChatGPT that enables users to access reviews, ratings, and images directly within their chat. The goal is to make trip planning highly personalized by combining all Tripadvisor content with OpenAI's advanced AI capabilities.

- **Perplexity Partnership**: In January 2025, Tripadvisor partnered with Perplexity to integrate its billion reviews and Viator's 300,000+ experiences with AI-powered search. This collaboration delivers personalized, actionable recommendations that simplify the trip-planning process.

Travelers who use Tripadvisor's generative AI tools spend **2 to 3 times more** than those using traditional interfaces. The AI not only simplifies planning but also surfaces better, more relevant options.
